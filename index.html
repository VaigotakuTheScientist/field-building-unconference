FAQ

What is AI safety?+

AI safety is the field working to make sure increasingly capable AI systems remain safe and beneficial. It spans technical research — like alignment, interpretability, and robustness — as well as governance and policy work shaping how these systems are built and deployed.

What is field building?+

Field building is the work of growing a field's capacity to deliver on its mission: attracting and developing talent, creating institutions and infrastructure, directing funding where it's needed, and connecting people so research turns into results. FBU is about doing exactly that for AI safety.

Who should attend?+

Anyone building the AI safety field — funders, founders, researchers, community organizers, and people who want to become one of them.

What is an unconference, and how does it work?+

An unconference flips the usual format: instead of a fixed programme, the schedule starts open and the participants fill it. Anyone can propose a talk, discussion, or workshop, and attendees simply join the sessions they care about. We believe this is the best way to release the latent creative energies in everyone attending.

Why does FBU focus on field building?+

There are already strong venues for AI safety research itself. What's missing is a place for the layer around the research — talent pipelines, funding, community infrastructure, and coordination. FBU focuses narrowly on that layer, because that's where a few days of the right conversations can move the whole field.

How does being online make FBU more accessible?+

Field-builders are spread across the globe. Being online removes travel costs, visas, and time-zone lock-in — anyone doing this work can attend from anywhere, at no cost, and join exactly the sessions that matter to them.
